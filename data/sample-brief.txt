Harborview's exclusivity was not ambiguous. "The Agreement was executed on March 15, 2020, granting Harborview exclusive distribution rights for Greenleaf products in the states of Oregon, Washington, Idaho, and Montana" (Termination Notice ¶ 8).

Three months into Year 3, Greenleaf's sales chief opened a parallel channel through Cascade and asked to keep it "informal and off the books for now" (GRN-004217 ¶ 4). Greenleaf's CEO then directed that the shipments be hidden: "Let's keep the Cascade shipments off the Harborview books" (GRN-004226 ¶ 35). By September, Greenleaf planned roughly $630K in Cascade shipments for the fall (GRN-004228 ¶ 45).

Greenleaf also choked supply through its quality process. The CEO told her QA director, "Let's keep it targeted to Harborview batches for now" (GL-PROD-004219 ¶ 16). The QA director reported back that "same criteria applied to Cascade lots would have flagged at least 2, but those weren't in the enhanced screening protocol" (GL-PROD-004220 ¶ 3).

When Harborview asked why shipments had fallen, Holcomb admitted internally, "I told him supply chain issues" (GRN-004230 ¶ 54). He projected Harborview's Year 3 purchases at about $5.2M (GRN-004230 ¶ 58). Greenleaf's CEO wrote to outside counsel, "We need to move on terminating Harborview" (GL-PROD-007834 ¶ 2).

Greenleaf sent its breach notice two months before Year 3 ended, claiming purchases of "approximately Four Million Two Hundred Seventy Thousand Dollars" (Breach Notice ¶ 41). In all, Greenleaf diverted approximately $1.9 million in product to Cascade (Compl. ¶ 13). Greenleaf declared that "the effective date of termination of the Agreement shall be March 17, 2023" (Termination Notice ¶ 7).
